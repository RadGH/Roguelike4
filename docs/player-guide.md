# Player guide

A short, spoiler-free introduction. The in-game codex and the [manual page](../manual.html)
cover everything else — and every unlock condition is meant to be read, so browsing them
spoils nothing.

## The one idea

Your weapons fire themselves. You never aim and you never press an attack button. Your
entire job is **where you stand** — away from what's telegraphed, near what you want to
collect, between danger and a downed friend.

## Controls

| Input | Keyboard | Gamepad | Touch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Move | WASD or arrows | left stick | drag anywhere |
| A — equipment item | Space | bottom face button | A button |
| B — movement item | Shift | right face button | B button |
| Pause | Esc | Start | — |

That's all of them.

## A run

1. Pick a class. Your first run has one choice; finishing it — win or lose — opens more.
2. Fight ten waves. Red shapes on the ground mean damage is about to land there; the
   bigger and slower the warning, the harder the hit.
3. Between waves: open what you found, pick a perk when you've leveled, and spend gold at
   the shop. **Weapons only come from the shop**, so gold is never optional.
4. Wave ten is the boss. Good luck.

## With friends

Two to four players share one screen — the camera pulls back to keep everyone in view.
Gold and experience are counted in full for each player (nobody splits anything), and
found items are dealt out in turns. If you drop, a teammate can stand next to you for a
few seconds to pull you back up; everyone lost returns at the end of the wave. The run
only ends if nobody is left standing.

## Losing is progress

Nothing you unlock ever makes your character start stronger — unlocks add new *options*:
classes, weapons, perks. Every condition is listed in the codex before you've met it, and
progress counts from every run, including the ones that go badly.
